# THE WORLD TODAY — Monday 23 November 2026

A numbers-led front page summing up 23 November 2026's world news in one
image: a big 10-number grid first, then the masthead, three hero scenes
(AI rout hits Asia, Guterres calls for AI rules, US lead over China at a
record low), 10 news cards, a 13-pin world map, 18 more stories, and a
red DATE CHECK box listing what did **not** happen on 23 November.

## How it is made

- `draw.py` renders `entry.png` (1024×1024, PIL).
- `main.py` (stdlib only) validates the PNG strictly and writes a
  byte-identical copy to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.

## Stories (31)

Heroes: Kospi −3.3% (4,708.54, −161 pts, intraday −5.8%) · Guterres:
regulate AI before it's too late (Bridgetown, 150 delegates) · US lead
over China at a record low (Asia Power Index 78.9 vs 73.6, gap 5.3).

Plus 10 cards and 18 more stories covering markets, tech, politics,
the Middle East, Africa and Europe — all dated Monday 23 November 2026.
Items labelled CLAIM are one side's claim, not confirmed fact.
